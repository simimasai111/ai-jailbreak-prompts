# Contributor Code of Conduct

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inclusive environment for everyone
contributing to or engaging with the LLM Jailbreak Taxonomy.

## Our Pledge

In the interest of fostering an open, harassment-free, and productive research
community, we pledge to make participation in this project a respectful experience
for everyone — regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex
characteristics, gender identity or expression, level of experience, education,
socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or
sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

**Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:**

- Using inclusive and welcoming language
- Respecting differing viewpoints and research approaches
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community and the research integrity of the project
- Showing empathy toward other community members
- **Verifying claims with primary sources before contributing**

**Examples of unacceptable behavior:**

- The use of sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- **Submitting attack patterns that include unsanitized harmful payloads** (see `DISCLOSURE.md`)
- **Misrepresenting unverified claims as verified** (see `METHODOLOGY.md` sourcing standards)
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional
  research setting

## Research Integrity Standards

This project applies additional standards for research contributions:

1. **Primary sources required**: Every cited claim must reference a primary source
   (paper, official documentation, or live repository). Affiliate review sites and
   secondary aggregators are not accepted as sources.

2. **Verbatim quotes for technical claims**: When citing specific numbers or rules,
   use verbatim quotes from the source. Paraphrasing is acceptable in summaries but
   not in the citation tables.

3. **Refuted claims documented**: If new evidence refutes a prior claim in the repo,
   the contribution must explicitly mark the prior claim as refuted in `CHANGELOG.md`.
   Silent corrections are not acceptable.

4. **Responsible disclosure**: New attack patterns are subject to the disclosure
   protocol in `DISCLOSURE.md`. Do not submit working exploit payloads.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards of
acceptable behavior. Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit,
or reject contributions that are not aligned with this Code of Conduct, or to ban
temporarily or permanently any contributor for behaviors deemed inappropriate.

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported
by opening an issue on GitHub or via the contact in `DISCLOSURE.md`. All complaints
will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly.
